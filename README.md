# Dehleez

Dehleez helps people in Pakistan prepare for government services. Ask a question in Urdu, Roman Urdu, or English to find a checklist of documents, application steps, and official sources.

## Live demo

**[Open the Gemini-connected app on Vercel](https://kaghaz-saathi.vercel.app/)**

Try `Mujhe CNIC renew karwana hai`, choose **Get my checklist**, and switch to Urdu to try **چیک لسٹ سنیں**.
